Findings:
1. Original Nike/Adidas/New Balance/Asics/Puma/Converse/Salomon/Hoka/On sneakers sell little on AliExpress
   (official "genuine/authentic" listings with a product code): AF1 top 36, Samba 52, Jordan 1 7, Air Max 97 1,
   NB 530 318. Copies with the logo don't come back from the partner API. Today's wordings already reach the
   best 4 in 26 of 30 sneaker models.
2. Many best sellers carry the model name without the brand, and only "model kind" finds them:
   F91W watch 2,128 / 1,010 sold (Casio-titled best: 72), Classic Wayfarer sunglasses 1,706 / 1,677 (Ray-Ban
   titled: 118), Tasman slippers 38 / 26 (4), a Define-style jacket 11,038 (5,210).
3. "brand kind" finds the brand's best listing the model search ranks out: NB 530 with 318 sold
   ("New Balance sneakers"; "New Balance 530" top 1-3 sold), JBL Flip 6 471 (206), Owala cup 129 (none).
4. Not on AliExpress at all: Dr. Martens 1460, Levi's 501, Tommy/Lacoste polos, Oral-B iO, GoPro Hero
   (only accessories "for" them), Nuptse 0-3 sold.
5. Traps "model kind" brings to the judges: Samba -> Latin dance shoes, Campus -> "campus style" shoes,
   "Watch 5" smartwatches for Seiko 5, a no-brand "Forerunner 570" (a Garmin copy, ₪92).
