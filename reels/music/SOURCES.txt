Only a 31 s section of each is kept here (the full songs stay on the owner's Mac, ~/Desktop/panda-reels/music/):
  ffmpeg -ss <sourceStart - 1> -i <source> -t 31 -af afade=t=out:st=30.5:d=0.5 -c:a aac -b:a 128k -ar 48000 -ac 2 <name>.m4a
beats.json, per file: bpm; phase (the first beat, start mod one beat); start = the section's first beat in the trimmed file (1.0 s, every beat after it is start + k*60/bpm);
sourceStart = the same moment in the full song. The section is 4 beats of lead-in, then 32 strong kicks (reels/beatgrid.py,
kick band, BPM refined to 0.01; the kicks over the used 17.5 s were checked to sit within 26 ms of the grid).
03 is the owner's hand pick (drop on the first cut); the others were picked by the same rule without listening.
06 Fashion Electro is left out: its low band has as many onsets on the off-beat as on the beat, so the grid could not be trusted.
